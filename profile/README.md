# Van Orton Recovery

Van Orton Recovery is a U.S. fraud recovery firm for victims of crypto and investment scams. We investigate, locate, and pursue the recovery of stolen crypto and funds.

## How a case works

1. **Free case assessment (usually within hours).** Tell us what happened through our short case form. You get a clear read on your recovery options, our recommended strategy and a quote for your case, always within one business day.
2. **Asset tracking and investigation (48 hours).** Once you engage us, we establish your full loss, trace the funds across blockchains and financial systems, and locate the institutions holding them.
3. **Recovery (around 60 days).** Starts within a week of engaging our services. We push the banks and exchanges holding your funds to freeze them and file your claim. You get a weekly status report throughout.

Every case is different. We take cases from $10,000.
